# Me Tri Dental Clinic

Get official information from Me Tri Dental Clinic (Nha Khoa Mễ Trì) in Me Tri,
Tu Liem, Hanoi, Vietnam, without leaving the conversation.

Ask about dental services and how each treatment works, typical duration and
recovery, reference cost ranges, the dentists and their experience, frequently
asked questions, patient reviews from Google, dental care articles, nearby
Hanoi areas and directions to the clinic, opening hours and contact details.
When you are ready, get a link to book an appointment on the clinic website.

Information is available in Vietnamese, English and German. It is educational
and does not replace an in-person dental examination.

## Try asking

- "What dental services does Me Tri Dental Clinic offer?"
- "How much does a dental implant cost at Me Tri Dental Clinic?"
- "How do I get to Me Tri Dental Clinic from My Dinh?"

## Good to know

- Prices are reference ranges in VND. The final cost is confirmed after an
  examination at the clinic.
- The plugin is read-only. Booking happens on
  [nhakhoametri.vn](https://nhakhoametri.vn/), not in chat, and the plugin cannot
  change or cancel appointments.
- No account is needed. The plugin connects to the clinic's server at
  `https://nhakhoametri.vn/mcp`. It sends only what each lookup needs: the
  language, a service or dentist identifier, an area name or search text. It does not
  ask for or store personal or health information, apart from standard
  technical server logs. See the
  [privacy policy](https://nhakhoametri.vn/en/chinh-sach-bao-mat) and
  [terms](https://nhakhoametri.vn/en/dieu-khoan-dieu-kien).
- Need help? [Contact the clinic](https://nhakhoametri.vn/en/lien-he).
